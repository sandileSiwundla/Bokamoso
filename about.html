About us

Developing youth and the community at large.

Since 2018, Bokamoso Elisha Noma Development Skills and Other Projects has helped young people in Soweto build the practical skills and experience that open doors to work.

Our story

A local organisation with a practical answer

We started in 2018 in Moroka, Soweto. Our focus is young people who have a TVET qualification but not yet the work experience employers ask for.

Our answer is practical: a work readiness programme where interns learn by doing everyday office, finance and compliance work at our office, alongside our team.

Founder’s story: who started Bokamoso, what led them to it, and how the organisation has grown since 2018.

What’s in a name

A name that looks forward

Our name carries our purpose. We exist to help young people build a future they can be proud of, starting with a fair first step into the working world.

The story behind “Elisha Noma”: who or what the name honours, and why it was chosen.

Bokamoso

“the future” in Setswana and Sesotho

For us, the future is something you build: one skill, one internship and one young person at a time.

How we work

What guides our work

Learning by doing

Skills stick when you use them. Our interns learn through practical, day-to-day office work, not just theory.

Close to home

We’re based in Moroka, close to the young people and families we serve, and part of the community we work in.

Work-ready, not just trained

We focus on the skills, habits and confidence that help graduates find work and do it well.
