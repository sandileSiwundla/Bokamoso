Work readiness internship

Get the experience employers are asking for.

A hands-on internship for TVET students and graduates, based at our office in Moroka, Soweto. Learn practical office, finance and compliance skills by doing the work.

What you’ll learn

Six skill areas, learned on the job

You’ll build experience across the work that keeps businesses and non-profits running: the kind of experience you can talk about in an interview and use from your first day at work.

  1. 01

    Office administration

    Filing, correspondence, scheduling, record-keeping and the professional habits that make an office run smoothly.

  2. 02

    NPO & company registration

    How non-profits and companies are formally set up: preparing the documents and following each registration step.

  3. 03

    NPO & company compliance

    Keeping organisations in good standing with statutory records, annual returns and reporting deadlines.

  4. 04

    Bookkeeping

    Capturing transactions, reconciling accounts and keeping accurate, up-to-date financial records.

  5. 05

    Books review

    Checking financial records for accuracy and completeness before they go to an accountant or auditor.

  6. 06

    Auditing

    Supporting audit preparation: gathering evidence, checking documents and following an audit trail.

Who can apply

Is this internship for you?

You don’t need work experience. This programme exists to give you that experience.

  • You’re studying at, or have graduated from, a TVET college
  • You’re interested in office, finance or compliance work
  • You’re ready to show up, learn and take on real tasks
  • Any age, area or year-of-graduation requirements

Documents you may need

Have these ready. We’ll let you know what to send if your application moves forward.

  • A copy of your ID
  • Your TVET certificate or latest academic record
  • A short CV

No. The programme is designed to give TVET students and graduates their first real work experience.

Yes, TVET students are welcome to apply. Confirm any study-year requirements.
