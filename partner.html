Why it matters

A qualification is only the start.

South Africa has one of the highest youth unemployment rates in the world. Many young people complete a TVET qualification and still struggle to find a first job, because employers want experience they haven’t yet had the chance to get.

Our work readiness programme closes that gap. We train interns in skills every organisation needs, from administration and bookkeeping to compliance and audit support, through hands-on work at our office in Moroka, Soweto.

How your support creates change

TVET students & graduates

Young people from Soweto with a qualification, but little or no workplace experience.

What we do

A hands-on internship

Practical training in six areas: office administration, registration, compliance, bookkeeping, books review and auditing.

Graduates with real experience, workplace confidence and skills employers need.
