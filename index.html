About us

Why we exist

Employers want experience. Graduates need somewhere to get it.

Every year, TVET colleges produce capable young people with the right qualifications. Many then face the same closed door: no experience, no job. And without a job, no experience.

Our work readiness programme breaks that cycle. Interns join us at our office in Moroka and build practical skills by doing the day-to-day work that every office depends on.

How the internship works

What interns learn

Six practical skills every office needs

Our training focuses on the everyday work that keeps businesses and non-profits running, and that employers need someone to do well.

See the full programme
01

Office administration

Filing, correspondence, scheduling and record-keeping: the habits that make an office run smoothly.

02

NPO & company registration

How organisations are formally set up, from preparing documents to following registration steps through to the end.

03

NPO & company compliance

Keeping organisations in good standing: statutory records, annual returns and reporting deadlines.

04

Bookkeeping

Capturing transactions, reconciling accounts and keeping accurate, up-to-date financial records.

05

Books review

Checking financial records for accuracy and completeness before they reach an accountant or auditor.

06

Auditing

Supporting audit preparation: gathering evidence, checking documents and following an audit trail.

The intern journey

How the internship works

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in a short online application with your details and your TVET qualification.

  2. 2

    Join the team

    Selected applicants join us as interns at our office on Vundla Street, Moroka.

  3. 3

    Learn by doing

    Build practical skills across administration, bookkeeping, compliance and auditing.

  4. 4

    Step into work

    Leave with real experience, workplace confidence and something to show employers.
